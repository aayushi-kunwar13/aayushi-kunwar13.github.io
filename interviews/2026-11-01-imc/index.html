  1. Tic-tac-toe: implement the game, including win and draw detection.

  3. Simple DSA question.

Notes

  • Tic-tac-toe in O(1) per move: keep counters for each row, column and both diagonals (+1 for X, -1 for O). A counter reaching ±n means a win; n×n moves with no win is a draw.
  • For the debugging part, check bounds, comparison operators, state resets and edge-case inputs first.
